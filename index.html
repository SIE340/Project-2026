Case & data

Your operating decision

Your company wants to meet the improvement targets in the first table while spending as little as possible each year. Decide how much to use each improvement option at each location, zone, or production line.

The company can use an option fully, partly, or not at all. The benefit and cost tables show what happens when an option is used fully at one location, zone, or production line. Benefits and costs change in proportion to how much of an option the company uses. For example, using it halfway gives half the listed benefits and costs half the listed amount. Benefits from the options used are added to find the total improvement.

The improvement targets are the minimum amounts to save or reduce each year compared with the company’s current operation. They are not limits on the amounts the company can consume or emit. Costs are listed in thousands of dollars per year; for example, a cost of 7.76 means $7,760 per year. Read the unit beside each improvement. The goal is to find the least expensive plan that meets all the requirements; no separate spending limit is given.
